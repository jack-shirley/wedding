03 / GETTING THERE

A little trip
to Telluride.

We're so excited to celebrate with you in one of our favorite mountain settings. Here are a few things to know as you plan your visit.

01 —

Getting Here

Telluride is nestled in southwest Colorado. Montrose Regional Airport (MTJ) is a common arrival option, about 65 miles away.

02 —

Where to Stay

Telluride and nearby Mountain Village offer a range of lodging options. Specific recommendations are coming soon.

03 —

What to Expect

August days are often mild and sunny, with cooler evenings. Pack a layer and comfortable shoes for mountain terrain.
